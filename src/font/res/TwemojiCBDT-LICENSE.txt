Twemoji (CBDT)
==============

The bundled `TwemojiCBDT.ttf` is the Twemoji color emoji font in
Google's CBDT/CBLC bitmap format, distributed by the Arch Linux
`ttf-twemoji-cbdt` package and originally produced from upstream
Twemoji image assets.

Source project: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji
                (formerly https://github.com/twitter/twemoji)
